# Catalog / Purchasing-Card Terms (Non-critical categories)

**Template for:** Non-critical-quadrant suppliers (low value, low risk), e.g. office supplies, MRO, paper, small tools, vehicle parts, test instruments. 1,522 kept suppliers and $312M per year in the case study.
**Goal:** make buying easy and cheap to administer. This is the route that replaces most of the 8,824 exited tail suppliers.

| Term | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Agreement form | Short-form catalog agreement accepted online; Buyer standard terms apply |
| Catalog | Supplier hosts a punch-out or uploaded catalog in Buyer's procurement system; only catalog items can be ordered |
| Pricing | Fixed catalog prices for `[12]` months; minimum `[x]%` below list price; reviewed yearly |
| Ordering | Through the procurement system or a purchasing card under `$[5,000]` per transaction |
| Delivery | `[2–5]` business days; free delivery above `$[x]` |
| Invoicing | Consolidated monthly e-invoice; purchasing-card transactions settled by the card provider |
| Payment | Net `[30]`; small and diverse suppliers net `[15]` |
| Service levels | Fill rate ≥ `[95]%`; returns accepted within `[30]` days |
| Reporting | Quarterly spend report by Buyer site |
| Term | `[2]` years, renewable; either party may end on `[30]` days' notice |
| Compliance | Supplier Code of Conduct acknowledgement at onboarding |

## Buying rules for employees

1. Use a catalog supplier when the item is in a catalog.
2. Use a purchasing card for non-catalog items under `$[5,000]`.
3. Anything else goes through a sourcing request. Setting up a new supplier needs a business justification (prevents the tail from regrowing).
