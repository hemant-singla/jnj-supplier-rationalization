# Master Supply / Services Agreement — Term Sheet (Leverage categories)

**Template for:** Leverage-quadrant suppliers (high value, competitive market), e.g. packaging, lab supplies, IT services, engineering and consulting, logistics. 1,648 suppliers and $3.9B per year (61% of kept spend) in the case study.
**Purpose:** consolidate each category to **2–3 preferred suppliers** and capture the volume leverage the business case depends on.
