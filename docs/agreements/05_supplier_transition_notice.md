## Notice A — Supplier record closure (R4 inactive, R5 one-time)

**Subject:** Update to your supplier account with `[Buyer]`

Dear `[Supplier contact]`,

Thank you for supplying `[Buyer]`. We are simplifying our supplier base, and as part of that we are closing supplier accounts that have had no recent purchase activity or were set up for a single purchase.

Your account `[supplier ID]` will be **closed on `[DATE, at least 60 days out]`**.

- All open purchase orders will be honored, and invoices for goods or services already delivered will be paid under the existing terms.
- No new purchase orders will be issued after `[DATE]`.
- If we need `[category]` items in future, we will buy them through our catalog and preferred-supplier programs.
- If you are a small or diverse business, you are welcome to apply to our supplier development program at `[link]`.

If you believe your account is still in active use, or you have open orders or invoices we have missed, please contact `[procurement contact]` by `[DATE]`.

Sincerely,
`[Name]`, `[Title]`, `[Buyer]` Procurement

## Notice B — Category consolidation (R8)

**Subject:** Changes to how `[Buyer]` buys `[category]`

Dear `[Supplier contact]`,

Thank you for your work with `[Buyer]`. We have completed a review of how we buy `[category]` and will consolidate this category with `[2–3]` preferred suppliers selected through a competitive process.

As a result, we expect to **stop placing new orders with your company after `[DATE, 90 days out]`**.

- **Wind-down period:** existing purchase orders and agreements will be honored until `[DATE]`. Please tell us if any work in progress needs longer.
- **Payment:** all invoices for delivered goods and services will be paid under current terms.
- **Transition support:** we would appreciate your help transferring specifications, open service tickets or item data by `[DATE]`; we will cover reasonable agreed costs.
- **Future opportunities:** you may take part in future sourcing events for this or other categories. Small and diverse businesses can join our supplier development program at `[link]`.

We know this is a significant change, and we're grateful for your service. Your contact `[name, email]` is available to answer questions.

Sincerely,
`[Name]`, `[Title]`, `[Buyer]` Procurement

## Notice C — Duplicate record merge (R3)

**Subject:** Your supplier records with `[Buyer]` are being combined

Dear `[Supplier contact]`,

Our records show your company under more than one supplier account (`[ID 1]`, `[ID 2]`). To make ordering and payment simpler, we will combine them into a single account, **`[master ID]`**, on `[DATE]`.

- **Nothing changes in our relationship**, your contracts or your prices.
- Please confirm your remittance details for `[master ID]` by `[DATE]` and send future invoices to that account.
- Open purchase orders on the old accounts will be moved automatically.

Thank you,
`[Name]`, `[Title]`, `[Buyer]` Procurement Operations
