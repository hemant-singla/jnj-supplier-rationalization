# Memorandum of Understanding — Strategic Supplier Partnership

**Template for:** Strategic-quadrant suppliers (high value, high supply risk), e.g. API and biologics-input makers and contract manufacturers. 417 suppliers in the case study.
**Pairs with:** a binding supply agreement and, for regulated materials, a GMP quality agreement.

---

**This Memorandum of Understanding** ("MOU") is entered into on `[DATE]` between:

- `[Buyer legal name]`, with offices at `[address]` ("Buyer"), and
- `[Supplier legal name]`, with offices at `[address]` ("Supplier"),

each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to build a long-term strategic partnership for `[category / materials / services]` that secures supply continuity, improves quality and total cost, and supports joint innovation for the patients and customers Buyer serves.

## 2. Nature of this MOU

2.1 This MOU records the Parties' **shared intent**. Except for Sections 9 (Confidentiality), 10 (Costs) and 12 (Governing law), it is **not legally binding** and creates no obligation to buy or supply.
2.2 Prices, volumes, delivery, quality and liability are governed only by the **Supply Agreement** dated `[DATE]` (and the Quality Agreement where applicable). If this MOU conflicts with those agreements, they prevail.

## 6. Information sharing

The Parties will share rolling forecasts (Buyer) and capacity, lead-time and risk information (Supplier) as set out in Annex A.

## 7. Term and review

This MOU runs for **`[3]` years** from signature and is reviewed each year at the executive sponsor review. Either Party may end it with `[90]` days' written notice; ending this MOU does not affect the Supply Agreement.

## 8. Exclusivity

Nothing in this MOU creates exclusivity. Buyer keeps the right to qualify additional sources to protect supply continuity.

## 9. Confidentiality (binding)

The confidentiality agreement dated `[DATE]` applies to all information shared under this MOU.

## 10. Costs (binding)

Each Party bears its own costs unless a joint project is agreed in writing.

## 11. Compliance

Each Party will comply with applicable law, including anti-bribery, sanctions, data protection, and Buyer's Supplier Code of Conduct.
