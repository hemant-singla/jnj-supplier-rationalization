# Long-term Supply Agreement — Term Sheet (Bottleneck categories)

**Template for:** Bottleneck-quadrant suppliers (lower value, high supply risk), e.g. specialty excipients, single-source device components, niche equipment and services. 1,061 suppliers and $791M per year in the case study.
**Goal:** secure supply and reduce dependence. **Not** price reduction: step 5 found small suppliers in these categories were *cheaper* than the kept ones (median −10%).

| Term | Proposed position | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Term | `[3–5]` years, auto-renewing yearly unless either party gives `[12]` months' notice | Long horizon matches requalification lead times (12–24 months for regulated inputs) |
| Supply commitment | Supplier reserves capacity for `[110]%` of Buyer's rolling 12-month forecast | Protects against allocation in a shortage |
| Buyer commitment | Minimum purchase of `[x]%` of forecast, or take-or-pay of `[y]` units/yr | Gives supplier reason to reserve capacity |
| Pricing | Fixed for `[2]` years, then indexed to `[raw-material index]`, capped at `[x]%`/yr | Price stability over price reduction |
| Safety stock | Supplier holds `[8–12]` weeks of finished stock (consignment option at Buyer site) | Buffer while a second source is qualified |
| **Last-time-buy / discontinuation** | `[12]` months' written notice before discontinuing any item; last-time-buy right at current price | Bottleneck items have few or no alternatives |
| **Change control** | No change to process, site, formulation or sub-supplier without `[6]` months' notice and Buyer approval | Regulated materials need requalification |
| **Second-source plan** | Buyer may qualify an alternate source; supplier provides specifications and samples needed for qualification | Removes single-source dependency over time |
| Sub-tier transparency | Supplier discloses critical sub-tier suppliers and sites | Hidden single points of failure |
| Business continuity | Business continuity plan reviewed yearly; notify Buyer of any disruption within `[24]` hours | — |
| Quality | Quality agreement (GMP where applicable) | Patient safety |
| Performance | OTIF ≥ `[98]%`; zero critical quality deviations | — |
| Exit assistance | On termination, supplier supports transfer to an alternate source for `[12]` months | Avoids stranded supply |
| Termination | Buyer for convenience on `[12]` months' notice; for cause after a cure period | — |

## Second-source roadmap (per bottleneck item)

| Step | Owner | Target timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify candidate alternate suppliers | Category manager | Month 0–3 |
| Technical and quality assessment | Quality / R&D | Month 3–6 |
| Samples, trials, stability data | Supplier + Buyer | Month 6–15 |
| Regulatory filing / change approval (if required) | Regulatory affairs | Month 12–24 |
| Split volume `[80/20]` across two sources | Procurement | After approval |
